Board-game mode ideas

10 new modes for Menagerie Chess that play like an abstract board game. Our own designs: they borrow feelings (building up, walling off, territory, racing), not other games' rules. Ideas only, nothing built. Board sketches are rough, Land at the bottom.

What all 10 share (defaults, easy to change)

  • 6 animals per side, one of each kind, like Sumo and Raid.
  • No die, cards or duels: everything is visible, like the genre. Luck could return later as an optional switch.
  • Powers: once per game, and using one is your whole turn. Each idea re-themes them.
  • Online: the server runs the rules; nothing is secret. The 60s turn timer works for all. Land moves first.
  • Size: S about a week across streams, M a normal phase, L a big phase.

My top 3 (you decide)

  1. Stepping Stones. Best reuse: sinking tiles already exist, so it mostly needs "rise" as well. Simple rules, a strong bot from a simple measure, short games. It feels the most like a real abstract board game.
  2. Shore Shift. The most on-theme: Land and Sea fight over the shoreline itself, and the board shows the score. Animals and powers work as they are.
  3. High Tide. The most dramatic: water creeping toward the dog house. A bigger balance job (the two sides play differently), so it may suit a second release.

Cheapest to try first: Paw Prints or Slick Sand (both S). Once you pick, core builds a 2D playtest first, like Phases 3 and 4, then the 3D build.
