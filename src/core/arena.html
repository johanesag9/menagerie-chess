Arena modes playtest

Dev tool for Sumo, Raid, Sandcastle King and Stepping Stones. Flat 2D board with emoji stand-ins, not the real game look.

    Mode
    Tuning (all four modes, applies to new games)
    How to play
    Raw events (newest first)

      Dots = steps. Red rings = shoves (Sumo) or tags (Raid), with their AP cost. Violet = power targets. Blue = water. Dashed wobbling squares sink at the end of this round. 🦴 Land's bone, 🦪 Sea's pearl; a small badge on an animal means it carries one. 🥣 / 🐚 = home squares. ⚓ dug in, 💫 stunned. 🏺 / ⚫ = blocks. A gold dot = power still ready. Sandcastle King: the number on a square is its level (darker = higher), 🏰 = a castle (level 3), 👑 = an animal standing on one. Green rings = squares to build on (+1 / +2), brown rings = squares to dig (−1). Stepping Stones: blue = water, 🫧 = changed last turn (can't be raised or sunk now). Green rings = raise, brown rings = sink.
